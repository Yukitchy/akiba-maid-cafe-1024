Lunch at a maid cafe: the plan

Hi Matt and Brian! You asked where we'd go for lunch, so here it is. We'll head to one main spot, and I have three backups in case it's packed or you'd like a different kind of place. We can decide together on the day.

The plan

This is where I'd like to take you.

First choice

Maidreamin Heaven's Gate

めいどりーみん 秋葉原 LIVE RESTAURANT Heaven's Gate

Maidreamin is the biggest maid cafe chain in Japan, and Heaven's Gate is its largest shop in Akihabara, with 60 seats and a full stage. The maids take turns singing and dancing on it while you eat. Order the omurice (omelette rice) and a maid will draw on it in ketchup for you. If you want the full maid cafe experience on your first visit, this is it.

  • Where: 6F, Ichigo Akihabara Ekimae Bldg, 1-15-9 Sotokanda. About 10 seconds from the Electric Town exit of Akihabara Station (the white building with namco)
  • Saturday hours: 10:30–23:00
  • Good to know: Reservations are possible, so I'll try to hold seats for us ahead of time
Official page Open in Google Maps

Backups

Each of these feels different, and they're all a short walk from the rest of the tour.

For Steins;Gate fans

Cafe Mai:lish

カフェ メイリッシュ

This is the real cafe that May Queen Nyan-Nyan, where Faris works in Steins;Gate, was based on. If you've seen the show, you'll recognize it.

It has been open for more than 20 years and still has that classic Akihabara feel. They serve real food like omurice and curry, so it works fine as a lunch stop. It's a little north of the station, toward Suehirocho.

  • Where: 2F, FH Kyowa Square, 3-6-2 Sotokanda
  • Hours: 12:00–22:00
Official site Open in Google Maps
Calm and classic

Cure Maid Café

キュアメイドカフェ

Opened in 2001, and often called the first maid cafe in Akihabara. It's quiet, closer to an English-style tea room with maids, and the tea is taken seriously (the shop is certified by the Japan Tea Association). Good if you'd rather sit, talk and eat without a show going on. They have curry and omurice for lunch.

  • Where: 4F, Onoden Bldg, 1-2-7 Sotokanda
  • Hours: 11:00–20:00
Official site Open in Google Maps
Big and easy for first-timers

@home cafe (Main Store)

@ほぉ～むカフェ 本店

One of the best-known maid cafes in Akihabara, open for over 20 years. It takes up five floors and has an English menu. A safe pick if the other places are full.

  • Where: 3F–7F, Mitsuwa Bldg, 1-11-4 Sotokanda (the Don Quijote building)
  • Hours: 10:00–22:00
